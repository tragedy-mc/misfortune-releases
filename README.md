# 🔥 Calamity Client

**A lightweight PvP client that looks good, runs fast, and stays out of your way.**

Custom menus · Movable HUD · Built-in FPS boost · Animated partner capes

`Minecraft 26.1.2` · `Fabric` · `Client-side only`

## Why Calamity?

Most PvP clients make you pick between **features** and **frames**. Calamity gives you both.

- ⚡ **High FPS on any PC.** Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore and ImmediatelyFast are **built in**, so there's nothing extra to install.
- 🎯 **Made for PvP.** CPS, reach, combo counter, totem tracking, crosshair indicators, cart PvP tools and more.
- 🎨 **A cleaner game.** A crimson Calamity menu, an animated home screen and a HUD you can drag anywhere.
- 🪶 **Lightweight.** Every feature can be turned off, and nothing runs that you don't use.
- 🛡️ **Works on any server.** It's client-side only, and Calamity players find each other automatically with no account or server needed.

## ✨ Features

### ⚔️ Combat
- **CPS counter.** Left and right clicks per second.
- **Reach display.** How far away your target is.
- **Combo counter.** Hits in a row, plus your best combo of the session.
- **Totem & potion counters.** Know what you've got left without opening your inventory.
- **Low health warning.** A red pulse when you're in danger, at a threshold you choose.
- **Crosshair Addons.** Player and mob indicators, an elytra icon, a rocket count, and a hitmarker when your arrows land.
- **Hitboxes.** One click, no F3+B.

### 💣 Cart PvP
- **Fuse timers** above lit TNT and TNT minecarts, colour-coded green → yellow → red.
- **Cleaner explosions.** Cut blast particles so you can see after a detonation.
- **Totem pop tracker.** "✦ Steve popped 3 totems" in chat, plus pops-before-death.
- **Cart HUD.** TNT carts, rails, arrows and totems at a glance.

### 🖥️ HUD
- FPS · Ping · Coordinates · Keystrokes · Clock · Speed · Direction
- **Armor HUD** with colour-coded durability and a warning before a piece breaks
- **Potion effects** with icons and timers
- **Minimap.** A live top-down map of the area around you.
- **HUD Editor.** Drag and resize anything, with snapping and a grid. Your layout keeps its place on any resolution.
- **Style options.** Size, backgrounds, opacity, accent outlines and text shadow.

### 👁️ Visual
- **Custom crosshair.** 5 shapes, 7 colours, adjustable size, gap and thickness, an optional outline and a live preview.
- **Smooth zoom.** Hold **C**, scroll to zoom further, and it eases in and out like Zoomify.
- **Freelook.** Hold **Left Alt** to look around without turning.
- **Fullbright.** See in the dark.
- **Visual tweaks.** No pumpkin blur, a lower fire overlay, a lower shield, and the option to remove world, water or lava fog.
- **Borderless fullscreen.** Alt-tab without the flicker.

### 🌍 World & Utility
- **Waypoints.** Press **N** to mark a spot and see its name and distance in the world. Waypoints are saved per server and per world.
- **Shulker preview.** See what's inside a shulker box from its tooltip.
- **AppleSkin built in.** Saturation, food and health previews, plus nutrition values in tooltips.
- **Horse stats.** Speed, jump height and health.
- **Chat timestamps** in 12h or 24h.
- **Auto sprint.**

### 🚀 Performance
- **One-click presets:** Max FPS · Balanced · Quality · **Low-End PC**
- Calamity spots weaker hardware and offers the Low-End preset for you.
- **Background FPS cap** to save battery and heat while you're tabbed out.
- Hand-tune render distance, simulation distance, entity distance, particles and the FPS limit.

### 🧥 Capes & Badges
- **Animated partner capes** from creators **3nderBendr**, **_piza** and **SaphMC**: *Nah I'd Win, Eye, Moonlit, Pizza, Claw, Stardust, Golden Hour, Crown, Moon Tide.*
- **Wardrobe** with big previews you can spin around.
- **Nametag badges.** A Calamity logo next to other Calamity players in nametags and the tab list.
- Other Calamity players see your cape too, with no server or account needed.

## 📦 Installation

1. Install **[Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/)** (0.19.0 or newer) for **Minecraft 26.1.2**.
2. Download **[Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)** and put it in your `mods` folder.
3. Drop the **Calamity Client** `.jar` into your `mods` folder.
4. Launch the game with the Fabric profile. You'll need **Java 25+**.
5. Press **P** to open the Calamity menu.

> 💡 Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore and ImmediatelyFast are already included, so don't install them separately.
> Optional extras: **Entity Culling** and **ModernFix** for even more FPS.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is it allowed on servers?**
Calamity is client-side and only adds information and visuals. It has no automation and no combat advantage beyond what popular HUD mods provide. Always check your server's rules on client mods.

**Will it lower my FPS?**
Usually it does the opposite. The bundled performance mods typically give a big FPS boost over vanilla.

**How do other players see my cape?**
Calamity players recognise each other automatically through the game itself, with no account or login. If a server blocks it, the menu's **Badge Signal** status tells you.

**Where are my settings saved?**
In `.minecraft/config/calamityclient*.properties`. Settings auto-save as you change them.
